Common questions

Is this the official BLACKBARN website?

No. This is a demo concept built by KezTech Solution to show how a private events enquiry form could work. It is not the official site for BLACKBARN Restaurant.

Does sending this form book my event?

No. This is an enquiry, not a booking. On a live version, the team would confirm availability and next steps in writing.

What group sizes does this form cover?

Groups of 12 to 300, and buyouts to 500, as listed for BLACKBARN private events. Enter your best estimate if your final count is not set yet.

Can I still email or call instead?

Yes. This page simply adds an online option. Email and phone remain available for anyone who prefers them.
